## Rules of engagement

1. **Stay in scope.** Only attack the challenge targets provided by this event. The web training target runs on your own machine (`localhost`). Never touch the CTF platform itself, other players, or any real-world system.
2. **No flag sharing.** Do not share flags, solutions or hints with other teams while the event is running.
3. **No brute forcing the scoreboard.** Flag submissions are rate-limited; automated guessing will get your account locked.
4. **One account per person.** Team size and team rules are configured by the organizers.
5. **Be kind.** This is a learning environment. Help newcomers understand concepts without handing out answers.

## Flag format

Unless a challenge says otherwise, flags look like `flag{...}`. Flags in the starter pack are labeled `flag{example_...}` because they are public training samples.

## Scoring

Each challenge is worth the points shown on its card. Some hints cost points — the cost is shown before you unlock them. Ties are broken by whoever reached the score first.

## Getting help

Open the command palette with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>/</kbd>) and type `help` to navigate quickly. Contact the organizers for platform issues.
